Lynda Chidi

Lynda Chidi is the founder and director of Medholic Ltd and the product lead behind Spotit Wound Care.

Open Spotit | About Spotit | Contact Medholic Ltd

About Lynda

Lynda Chidi leads Medholic Ltd with a focus on practical healthcare technology, clinical workflow improvement, and digital tools that can support safer, clearer documentation conversations.

Her work on Spotit is focused on wound-care workflow: helping teams capture wound information, assess progress, plan reviews, coordinate caseloads, and prepare clearer reports.

Apps and Product Work

  • Spotit Wound Care: a digital wound-care workflow app for controlled demonstrations, buyer review, and future pilot discussions.
  • Medholic digital health concepts: healthcare and pharmacy workflow ideas focused on documentation, stock, reporting, coordination, and operational visibility.

Current Stage

Spotit Wound Care is currently positioned for private demonstration, controlled testing, buyer review, and governance preparation. It is not for live patient data unless the required governance, clinical safety, data protection, and local approvals are completed.
